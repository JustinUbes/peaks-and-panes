Accessibility Statement

We want every homeowner to be able to use our website, regardless of ability or technology.

Our Commitment

Peaks and Panes is committed to ensuring digital accessibility for people with disabilities. We are continually improving the user experience for everyone and applying the relevant accessibility standards to our website.

Conformance Standard

This website aims to conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at Level AA. These guidelines explain how to make web content more accessible to people with a wide range of disabilities, including visual, auditory, motor, and cognitive impairments.

Measures We Take

To support accessibility, our website:

  • Can be navigated fully by keyboard, including a "Skip to main content" link on every page.
  • Uses semantic page structure, headings, and landmark regions so screen readers can move through content efficiently.
  • Provides descriptive text alternatives for images.
  • Maintains sufficient color contrast between text and backgrounds.
  • Labels all form fields and clearly marks required information.
  • Respects your operating system's reduced-motion preference.
  • Is tested with automated accessibility tools and manual keyboard review.

Known Limitations

Our Windows and Doors pages include product catalogs provided by ProVia, a third-party manufacturer. We do not control the accessibility of that embedded content. If you have difficulty using it, the same product information is available by contacting us directly.

Feedback and Assistance

If you encounter an accessibility barrier on this website, or need information in an alternative format, please let us know. We will respond as soon as possible and do our best to provide the information or service you need.
